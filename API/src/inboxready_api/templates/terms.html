The service

InboxReady runs public DNS lookups against domains you submit and returns a scored assessment of their email authentication posture. It is diagnostic. It does not send mail, modify your DNS, or guarantee inbox placement.

Acceptable use

  • Audit domains you own, operate, or have been asked to help with.
  • Do not use the API to enumerate third-party infrastructure at scale for targeting.
  • Do not attempt to circumvent rate limits with rotating keys or addresses.
  • Do not resell raw API access as if it were your own service without agreement.

We may suspend a key that is doing any of the above, and we will tell you why.

Plans, volume, and overage

  • Each plan includes a monthly audit allowance; the full feature set is on every plan.
  • Exceeding the allowance bills overage at the plan's rate rather than hard-stopping calls.
  • Plans are month to month. Cancel and the current period runs out; we do not pro-rate.
  • Prices are in USD and exclude any tax that applies where you are.

Availability

We aim for continuous availability and publish {% if static_build %}liveness and readiness probes{% else %}liveness and readiness probes{% endif %} so you can verify it yourself. Audits depend on public DNS and on policy files published by third parties; when those are slow or unreachable, a check returns a warning rather than a false pass. No uptime figure is contractually promised on the self-serve plans.

Your data

Covered by the privacy policy. In short: audit inputs are not retained by default, and we do not build a product out of the domains you look up.

Liability

The service is provided as-is. A passing score means the checks we run found nothing wrong at that moment; it is not a warranty that mail will be delivered. To the extent the law allows, our aggregate liability is limited to the fees you paid in the three months before the claim.
