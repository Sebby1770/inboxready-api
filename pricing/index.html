Before you commit

Questions that come up at this point

Does an audit send email or touch my customers?Data

No. Every check is a public DNS lookup plus, for MTA-STS and BIMI, a fetch of a policy file the domain owner published deliberately. Nothing is delivered to a mailbox and no mail flow is affected.

How fast will someone answer me?Support

Within one business day, Monday to Friday, Melbourne time. Suspected outages jump the queue — and /healthz and /readyz will tell you instantly whether the service itself is the problem.

What happens if I go over my plan's audit volume?Pricing

Nothing breaks mid-month. Overage is billed at the plan's rate rather than being hard-stopped, because an audit usually fires at the exact moment a customer is trying to activate — a 402 there costs you far more than the overage does.
