Terms of service

Last updated 24 August 2026. These terms cover the hosted InboxReady API.

The service

InboxReady runs public DNS lookups against domains you submit and returns a scored assessment of their email authentication posture. It is diagnostic. It does not send mail, modify your DNS, or guarantee inbox placement.

Acceptable use

  • Audit domains you own, operate, or have been asked to help with.
  • Do not use the API to enumerate third-party infrastructure at scale for targeting.
  • Do not attempt to circumvent rate limits with rotating keys or addresses.
  • Do not resell raw API access as if it were your own service without agreement.

We may suspend a key that is doing any of the above, and we will tell you why.

Plans, volume, and overage

  • Each plan includes a monthly audit allowance; the full feature set is on every plan.
  • Exceeding the allowance bills overage at the plan's rate rather than hard-stopping calls.
  • Plans are month to month. Cancel and the current period runs out; we do not pro-rate.
  • Prices are in USD and exclude any tax that applies where you are.

Availability

We aim for continuous availability and publish liveness and readiness probes so you can verify it yourself. Audits depend on public DNS and on policy files published by third parties; when those are slow or unreachable, a check returns a warning rather than a false pass. No uptime figure is contractually promised on the self-serve plans.

Your data

Covered by the privacy policy. In short: audit inputs are not retained by default, and we do not build a product out of the domains you look up.

Liability

The service is provided as-is. A passing score means the checks we run found nothing wrong at that moment; it is not a warranty that mail will be delivered. To the extent the law allows, our aggregate liability is limited to the fees you paid in the three months before the claim. Nothing here excludes liability that cannot lawfully be excluded, including under the Australian Consumer Law.

Source code

The implementation is published under the MIT licence in the project repository. Those terms govern the code; these terms govern the hosted service.

InboxReady

InboxReady audits whether a customer-owned domain is ready to send trustworthy email. One API call checks MX, SPF, DKIM, DMARC, MTA-STS, TLS-RPT, and BIMI, then returns a weighted score with the exact DNS records to fix.

We reply within 1 business day. Monday to Friday, 09:00–17:00 Melbourne time.
